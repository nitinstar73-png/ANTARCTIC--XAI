ANTARCTIC-XAI❄️☃️
AI-Powered Antarctic Navigation & Explainable Risk Intelligence

ANTARCTIC-XAI is an AI-based prototype for Antarctic navigation and environmental risk intelligence. It combines environmental data, machine learning, and Explainable AI (XAI) to help visualize potential navigation risks and understand the factors behind predictions.


 🎯 Problem Statement

Antarctic navigation is challenging because environmental conditions can change rapidly due to sea ice, icebergs, weather, and ocean conditions.

ANTARCTIC-XAI aims to provide an intelligent interface that can:

- Identify potential navigation risk zones
- Analyze environmental conditions
- Generate machine-learning-based risk predictions
- Explain the factors influencing predictions
- Visualize information on an Antarctic map


🤖 Machine Learning

The project is designed around *Machine Learning* with
XGBoost
 as the planned/used prediction model.

Potential environmental features include:

- Sea-ice conditions
- Weather conditions
- Ocean conditions
- Iceberg-related information
- Satellite-derived features

The model can produce a risk probability that can be represented through different risk levels.



🔍 Explainable AI — SHAP

*SHAP (shapley additive explanations)* can be used to explain machine-learning predictions.

The XAI layer is intended to answer questions such as:

- Why is this area considered risky?
- Which factors influenced the prediction?
- Which factors increased or decreased the predicted risk?
